# Drive World

A small low-poly three.js driving game.

Drive around the island, collect 41 gold coins, hit boost pads, jump ramps, visit the windmill and lighthouse, and knock beach balls around.

## Controls

- Arrow keys or WASD: drive and steer
- Space: drift
- C: change camera distance
- R: reset the car
- Touch controls appear automatically on phones and tablets

Three.js is vendored in `lib/` under its MIT license so the game has no CDN dependency.
